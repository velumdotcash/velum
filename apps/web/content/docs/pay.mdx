# Pay

Send private payments using Velum payment links.

## Sending a Payment

<Steps>
<Step>
**Open the Payment Link**

Click the link you received to open the Velum payment page.
</Step>

<Step>
**Connect Your Wallet**

Connect any Solana wallet with enough of the requested token plus SOL for fees.
</Step>

<Step>
**Enter Amount**

If the amount isn't pre-set, enter how much to send.
</Step>

<Step>
**Confirm Payment**

Approve the transaction. Funds are sent privately to the recipient.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens

1. **Your wallet signs the transaction** — standard Solana approval
2. **Funds are shielded** — tokens are converted to private balance
3. **Recipient receives privately** — funds appear in their shielded account
4. **No public trace** — the blockchain shows no sender/recipient link

## Fees

| Fee Type | Cost |
|----------|------|
| Velum fee | 0.15% of deposit amount (same token) |
| Network fee | ~0.000005 SOL |
| Priority fee | ~0.0001 SOL |

The Velum fee is a separate transfer instruction included in the same transaction. You'll see it in your wallet's approval popup before confirming.

<Callout type="tip">
Keep a small amount of SOL in your wallet for gas fees, even when paying with USDC or USDT.
</Callout>
