# Receive Payments

Create a payment link and share it to receive funds privately.

## Creating a Payment Link

<Steps>
<Step>
**Connect Your Wallet**

Select your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, etc.) and sign the message to initialize your shielded account.
</Step>

<Step>
**Configure Your Link**

Choose token, amount, and memo options for your payment link.
</Step>

<Step>
**Share the Link**

Copy the generated URL or QR code and share it with the sender.
</Step>
</Steps>
