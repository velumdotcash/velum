## Why Velum?

On public blockchains, every transaction is visible. If someone knows your wallet, they can see everything: your balance, who pays you, who you pay, your entire financial history.

**This lack of privacy is the biggest barrier to mainstream crypto adoption.**

Velum changes this. Using Zero-Knowledge cryptography, we enable private transactions on Solana. Neither sender nor recipient reveal their wallet addresses.

## Use Cases

- **Freelancers** — Get paid without exposing your main wallet or financial history
- **Donations** — Accept contributions while protecting donor and recipient privacy
- **E-commerce** — Accept crypto payments without linking every sale to your business
- **Tips & Support** — Creators receive support without revealing their balance
- **Friends & Family** — Split expenses without awkward balance exposure

<Callout type="info">
Your funds are always secure. Velum is built on [Privacy Cash](https://privacycash.org), an audited ZK privacy protocol on Solana with proven infrastructure.
</Callout>
